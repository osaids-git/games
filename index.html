Take the scenic route. Every spark counts.

Reach the lighthouse at the end of five chapters. Jump over extra ground spikes and holes. The first chapter's tall rotating and pop-up spike gates cannot be cleared by jumping; wait for them to retract, then run through. Shoot each chapter boss to advance. The last chapter boss has 35 health. After the first chapter, the Shadow Hunter follows and fires until defeated by 20 spark hits. You have six lives per run; falling or taking a hit uses one. Flags save progress and remaining lives in this browser.
